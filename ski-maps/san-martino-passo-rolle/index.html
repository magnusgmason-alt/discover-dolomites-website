San Martino di Castrozza · Passo Rolle

Tognola, Ces, Colverde and Passo Rolle

54 kmRuns on this map
20Lifts
0Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

San Martino di Castrozza sits below the great walls of the Pale di San Martino, with ski areas on both sides of the valley and more open skiing up at Passo Rolle. It is a relaxed, traditional resort with a good park for learning freestyle.

Best for

Beginners, families and intermediates.

Highlights

  • Tognola, with a Burton progression park
  • Colverde, below the Pale di San Martino
  • The open slopes of Passo Rolle
  • Views of the Cimon della Pala

Where to stay

San Martino di Castrozza and Passo Rolle. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

Mountains in this area

  • Cimon della Pala

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.

Our map is an illustrated panorama drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is for planning only. Run lengths differ from official figures. Always check the official piste map and run and lift status on the day.
