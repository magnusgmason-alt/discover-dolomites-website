Alta Badia

Corvara, Colfosco, La Villa and San Cassiano

145 kmRuns on this map
44Lifts
5Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

Alta Badia is the gentlest corner of the Sella Ronda: wide, well-groomed, mostly blue and red runs between Corvara, Colfosco, La Villa and San Cassiano, with a big choice of ski schools and some of the best hut food in the Alps.

Best for

Beginners and intermediates, families, and anyone who loves long cruising runs and great food.

Highlights

  • The Gran Risa in La Villa, a World Cup giant slalom slope
  • Corvara’s Boè gondola straight onto the Sella Ronda
  • Piz Sorega and the wide pistes above San Cassiano
  • Mountain huts famous for their cooking

Where to stay

Corvara, Colfosco, La Villa, San Cassiano, Badia and La Val. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

Mountains in this area

  • Piz Boè
  • Sassolungo

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.

Our map is an illustrated panorama drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is for planning only. Run lengths differ from official figures. Always check the official piste map and run and lift status on the day.
