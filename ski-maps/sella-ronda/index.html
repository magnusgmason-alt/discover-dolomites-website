Sella Ronda

The classic ski circuit around the Sella massif

437 kmRuns on this map
128Lifts
5Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

The Sella Ronda is the classic ski tour of the Dolomites: a loop of lifts and pistes around the Sella massif, through Val Gardena, Alta Badia, Arabba and Val di Fassa. You can ski it in either direction on the Dolomiti Superski pass.

Best for

Confident intermediates with the stamina for a long day.

Highlights

  • About 44 km around the Sella massif in a day
  • Four passes: Sella, Pordoi, Campolongo and Gardena
  • Orange signs clockwise, green signs anticlockwise
  • Start by 10:00 at the latest

Where to stay

Selva, Corvara, Arabba and Canazei (and the villages of all four valleys). Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

Mountains in this area

  • Piz Boè
  • Sassolungo
  • Marmolada

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.

Our map is an illustrated panorama drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is for planning only. Run lengths differ from official figures. Always check the official piste map and run and lift status on the day.
