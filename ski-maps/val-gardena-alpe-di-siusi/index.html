Val Gardena · Alpe di Siusi

Seceda, Ciampinoi, Dantercepies and the Seiser Alm

A woodcarvers' valley beneath the Sassolungo, a World Cup downhill, a 10.5 km run from Seceda to Ortisei and, over the ridge, the gentlest high plateau in the Alps: Dolomiti Superski's biggest area.

Val Gardena begins where the road from the Isarco valley climbs into a world of steep meadows, church spires and three Ladin villages strung along the valley floor. Above them the Sassolungo (Langkofel) rises to 3,181 m, and on the far side of the valley the jagged Odle peaks frame the Seceda ridge.

Over the shoulder of the Sassolungo lies the Alpe di Siusi (Seiser Alm), 56 km² of rolling pasture that is Europe's largest high alpine meadow. One ski pass covers both: steep, fast Val Gardena and the open, sunny plateau where families learn to ski.

Best forAll levels. Gentle Alpe di Siusi for families and beginners; Ciampinoi and the Saslong for strong skiers.

The Area

Two Worlds, One Pass

Together, Val Gardena and the Alpe di Siusi form what Dolomiti Superski calls its largest region, with about 175 km of slopes served by 79 lifts. Most of it is red: of the 175 km, 105 km are intermediate, 52 km easy and 18 km black. Val Gardena is a deep valley of long, steep descents through forest; the Alpe di Siusi a high, open plateau of about 59 km of mostly gentle slopes.

The valley's largest village and its woodcarving capital. Three lift systems leave from town: the cable car towards Seceda, the gondola up to the Alpe di Siusi, and the Resciesa funicular, an eight-minute ride to 2,200 m and the start of a 6 km toboggan run (see beyond skiing).

Tip Ski La Longia late in the day and you finish in Ortisei, a short walk from the cafés.

The smallest of the three, in the middle of the valley, with the Saslong World Cup piste on the slopes above. The Col Raiser gondola climbs towards the Seceda slopes, while Monte Pana is a sunny shelf with a 35 km cross-country network linked to the Alpe di Siusi trails.

Best Runs

The Legendary Eight

The tourist board bundles its classics as The Legendary 8, 36 km of runs you can track by GPS for a diploma. The headline act is La Longia: 10.5 km and 1,273 m of descent from Seceda through forest and a natural canyon to Val d'Anna in Ortisei, the longest slope in South Tyrol. The Saslong is the men's World Cup downhill (3,446 m as a race course, 3,750 m as a public piste), with its famous Camel Humps; on 18 and 19 December 2026 it hosts a super-G and a downhill. The Gardenissima, 6 km from Seceda to the Col Raiser valley station, is used each spring for a giant slalom billed as the world's longest.

Ciampinoi no. 3, the steepest of the eight, is being reshaped for the 2031 Alpine World Ski Championships, which return to Val Gardena after 61 years. Full run profiles are in our guide to famous runs.

The northern rim of the ski area, inside the Puez-Odle Nature Park, faces the spiky Odle and across to the Sassolungo and Sella. Seceda is the start of both La Longia and the Gardenissima, and on clear mornings the view sweeps from the Odle to the Sella.

Tip Go up first thing: the long runs down are best on fresh corduroy.

Opened

Built by Leitner for winter 2013, this two-section gondola lifts 3,000 people an hour from Selva towards Passo Gardena, the clockwise Sella Ronda gateway to Colfosco and Corvara. Coming back, the black Cir drops 675 m to the Vallunga valley station.

From Ortisei a gondola links to the Alpe di Siusi, and La Pilat, 4.7 km through forest, returns to the valley. From Monte Pana a ski bus runs to Saltria, at the eastern edge of the plateau.

Alpe Di Siusi

The Gentle Plateau

The plateau feels like another country: wide, sunny slopes, traditional huts and the Sciliar (Schlern) on the skyline. From Siusi (Seis) the Seiser Alm cable car reaches Compaccio (Compatsch) in about 15 minutes, and private cars may drive up only at set times. On the plateau itself skiers ride only chairlifts and drags, and overpasses carry the pistes across the roads, so skis rarely come off before lunch.

The plateau's headline investment for the new season replaces a slow 1994 double with an eight-seater. It is part of Dolomiti Superski's investment of about €100 million for 2026/27.

Children have the Sammy and Saltria kiddylands, families the Witches' Flight, an app-based ski escape game, and freestylers the Alpe di Siusi Snowpark (snowboarding and snowparks and skiing with kids).

Who It Suits

Choosing Your Base

  • Strong intermediates and experts: Selva, for the Sella Ronda, Ciampinoi and the Saslong.
  • Mixed groups and non-skiers: Ortisei, with the plateau, La Longia and a real town on the doorstep.
  • Beginners and young families: the Alpe di Siusi villages or Compaccio, on gentle snow.
  • Cross-country skiers: Santa Cristina and Monte Pana (more in beyond skiing).

Local tip

  • Buy your pass online at least two days ahead for 5% off (more in ski passes and prices).
  • World Cup weekend (18–19 December 2026) is festive but busy; book early (where to stay: our guide to where to stay).
  • Leave Seceda by mid-afternoon if you want La Longia to Ortisei: it is long.

In short
