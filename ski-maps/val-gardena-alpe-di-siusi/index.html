Val Gardena · Alpe di Siusi

Seceda, Ciampinoi, Dantercepies and the Seiser Alm

233 kmRuns on this map
73Lifts
13Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

Val Gardena and Alpe di Siusi together make one of the largest and most varied ski areas in the Dolomites, below the Sassolungo, the Sella and the Odle. Selva and Santa Cristina sit right on the Sella Ronda, Ortisei has the big views from Seceda, and the high plateau of Alpe di Siusi is a paradise for families.

Best for

All levels. Gentle Alpe di Siusi for families and beginners; Ciampinoi and the Saslong for strong skiers.

Highlights

  • The Saslong in Santa Cristina, home of the World Cup downhill
  • La Longia, the long run from Seceda down to Ortisei
  • The wide, sunny slopes of Alpe di Siusi, linked to Ortisei by the Mont Seuc gondola
  • Direct access to the Sella Ronda from Selva and Plan de Gralba

Where to stay

Ortisei, Santa Cristina, Selva, Castelrotto and Siusi. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

Mountains in this area

  • Sassolungo
  • Seceda
  • Sass Rigais
  • Sciliar · Schlern

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.

Our map is an illustrated panorama drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is for planning only. Run lengths differ from official figures. Always check the official piste map and run and lift status on the day.
