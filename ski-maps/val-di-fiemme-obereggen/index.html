Val di Fiemme · Obereggen

Obereggen, Pampeago, Predazzo and Cermis

118 kmRuns on this map
31Lifts
11Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

Val di Fiemme and Obereggen spread around the Latemar, with Obereggen, Pampeago and Predazzo linked into one area and Cermis above Cavalese. Obereggen is one of the best places in the Dolomites for families and park riders.

Best for

All levels; Obereggen is great for families and freestyle.

Highlights

  • Obereggen’s snowpark and night skiing
  • The Latemar ski area linking Obereggen, Pampeago and Predazzo
  • Cermis above Cavalese, with long runs down to the valley
  • Quiet slopes and good snow on the Latemar’s northern side

Where to stay

Obereggen, Pampeago, Predazzo and Cavalese. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

Mountains in this area

  • Latemar
  • Catinaccio · Rosengarten

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.

Our map is an illustrated panorama drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is for planning only. Run lengths differ from official figures. Always check the official piste map and run and lift status on the day.
