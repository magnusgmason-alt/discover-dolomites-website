Kronplatz · Plan de Corones

One mountain, lifts on every side

84 kmRuns on this map
23Lifts
15Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

Kronplatz (Plan de Corones) is a single rounded mountain above Brunico with lifts and runs on every side. It is one of the best-organised ski mountains in the Alps, easy to navigate and quick to ski from any base village.

Best for

All levels: wide red and blue runs on every side, plus a group of famous black runs.

Highlights

  • Lifts from every side to a single summit
  • The ‘Black Five’, the mountain’s five black runs
  • A large snowpark with lines for every level
  • Museums on the summit, including the Messner Mountain Museum Corones

Where to stay

Brunico, San Vigilio di Marebbe, Valdaora and Riscone. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

Mountains in this area

  • Kronplatz

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.
