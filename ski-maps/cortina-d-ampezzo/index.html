Cortina d’Ampezzo

Tofane, Cinque Torri, Lagazuoi and Faloria-Cristallo

102 kmRuns on this map
29Lifts
0Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

Cortina d’Ampezzo, the ‘Queen of the Dolomites’, is ringed by three ski areas: the Tofane, Cinque Torri-Lagazuoi and Faloria-Cristallo. It is as much about the setting as the skiing, with some of the most spectacular rock scenery in the Alps.

Best for

All levels, from the gentle Socrepes slopes to the Olympic runs of the Tofane.

Highlights

  • The Olympia delle Tofane, a World Cup and Olympic slope
  • Cinque Torri and Lagazuoi, with the long run to Armentarola in Alta Badia
  • Faloria-Cristallo, quiet runs with views of the Sorapiss
  • Host of the Winter Olympics in 1956 and 2026

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.
