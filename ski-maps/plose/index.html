Plose · Bressanone

The Plose, Bressanone’s own mountain

25 kmRuns on this map
8Lifts
23Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

The Plose is Bressanone’s own mountain: wide open runs on its western side, long descents through the forest and big views to the Odle. With Gitschberg Jochtal it makes up the Rio Pusteria–Bressanone area of Dolomiti Superski.

Best for

Families, intermediates and tobogganers.

Highlights

  • The Trametsch, one of the longest runs in South Tyrol
  • A long toboggan run
  • Views of the Odle and the Alps
  • A gondola from Sant’Andrea, just above Bressanone

Where to stay

Bressanone (Brixen) and Sant’Andrea. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

Mountains in this area

  • Sass Rigais

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.

Our map is an illustrated panorama drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is for planning only. Run lengths differ from official figures. Always check the official piste map and run and lift status on the day.
