3 Zinnen Dolomites

Monte Elmo, Croda Rossa and Sesto

138 kmRuns on this map
19Lifts
17Toboggan runs
1Superski pass

3 Zinnen Dolomites is the ski area of the Sesto Dolomites, with Monte Elmo and Croda Rossa above Sesto and San Candido. It takes its name from the Tre Cime di Lavaredo, just over the ridge.

Best for

Families and intermediates.

Highlights

  • Monte Elmo and Croda Rossa, linked by a ski connection
  • Long forest runs with views of the Sexten Dolomites
  • Monte Elmo’s link across the border to Sillian in Austria
  • Easy access from the Val Pusteria railway

Where to stay

Sesto, Moso, San Candido and Dobbiaco. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

Mountains in this area

  • Tre Cime di Lavaredo
  • Cadini di Misurina

Before you go

Helmets and third-party liability insurance are compulsory on Italian slopes. Read the ski rules, check what children pay, and if you are new to skiing start with our beginner’s guide.

Our map is an illustrated panorama drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is for planning only. Run lengths differ from official figures. Always check the official piste map and run and lift status on the day.
