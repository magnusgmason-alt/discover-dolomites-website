Sciliar

Sciliar · Schlern

2,563 m · The Dolomites

2,563 mHeight
SciliarGroup
46.506°N11.605°E

The flat-topped symbol of South Tyrol, rising above Alpe di Siusi and Castelrotto.

How to see it

The flat-topped Sciliar dominates the views from Alpe di Siusi and Castelrotto. Paths climb from the Alpe to Rifugio Bolzano on the summit plateau, below the highest point, Monte Pez.

Where to stay

Castelrotto, Siusi and Fiè allo Sciliar. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

When to go

Summer and autumn for the summit; in winter it is the backdrop to the gentle slopes of Alpe di Siusi.

Good to know

The Sciliar is the symbol of South Tyrol, and part of the Sciliar-Catinaccio nature park. The slender Santner spire stands at its western edge.
