Nuvolau

Averau

2,649 m · The Dolomites

2,649 mHeight
NuvolauGroup
46.500°N12.035°E

A blocky summit above the Cinque Torri with a short via ferrata and huge views from Rifugio Averau.

How to see it

Rifugio Averau sits at the foot of the summit, reached from the Cinque Torri chairlift or from Passo Falzarego. A short via ferrata climbs to the top; nearby Rifugio Nuvolau has one of the finest panoramas in the Dolomites.

Where to stay

Cortina d’Ampezzo. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

When to go

Summer for the via ferrata; in winter the huts are on the ski slopes of the Cinque Torri area.

Good to know

The Averau is the highest point of the Nuvolau group, above the Cinque Torri.
