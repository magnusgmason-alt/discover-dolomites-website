Cadini

Cadini di Misurina

2,839 m · The Dolomites

2,839 mHeight
CadiniGroup
46.596°N12.255°E

A forest of jagged spires above Lake Misurina, seen at its best from the Cadini viewpoint path.

How to see it

The famous viewpoint over the Cadini spires is a short but exposed walk from near Rifugio Auronzo, on the path towards Rifugio Fonda Savio. Lake Misurina below gives the classic reflection.

Where to stay

Misurina, Auronzo and Cortina d’Ampezzo. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

When to go

Summer and early autumn. The viewpoint path is narrow and very busy: go early, and keep well back from the edge.

Good to know

The Cadini are a maze of more than a dozen sharp summits, dominated by Cima Cadin di San Lucano.
