Civetta

Civetta

3,220 m · The Dolomites

3,220 mHeight
CivettaGroup
46.380°N12.053°E

Its north-west face, a wall more than a kilometre high, rises straight above Lake Alleghe: one of the great walls of the Alps.

How to see it

The north-west face rises more than a kilometre above Lake Alleghe: the classic view is from the lake shore. A popular walk leads from the Ski Civetta lifts to Rifugio Coldai and the little Lago Coldai below the wall.

Where to stay

Alleghe, Selva di Cadore, Zoldo and Pescul. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

When to go

In winter the Ski Civetta area links Alleghe, Zoldo and Selva di Cadore. In summer the Civetta is ringed by hut-to-hut paths, including stages of the Alta Via 1.

Good to know

Climbers call the north-west face the ‘wall of walls’. It is one of the great faces of the Alps.
