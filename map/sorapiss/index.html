Sorapiss

Sorapiss

3,205 m · The Dolomites

3,205 mHeight
SorapissGroup
46.517°N12.217°E

Hides the turquoise Lago di Sorapis, one of the most popular hikes from Passo Tre Croci.

How to see it

The famous turquoise Lago di Sorapis lies at the foot of the Sorapiss, reached on path 215 from Passo Tre Croci. Allow about two hours each way, with some exposed sections secured with cables.

Where to stay

Cortina d’Ampezzo and Misurina. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

When to go

Summer and early autumn. The lake path is very busy in July and August: start early.

Good to know

Rifugio Vandelli stands near the lake and is a good place for lunch before the walk back.
