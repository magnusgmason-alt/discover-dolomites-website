Tofane

Tofana di Mezzo

3,244 m · The Dolomites

3,244 mHeight
TofaneGroup
46.537°N12.069°E

Three huge summits above Cortina. The Freccia nel Cielo cable car climbs almost to the top of Tofana di Mezzo.

How to see it

The Freccia nel Cielo (‘Arrow in the Sky’) cable car climbs from Cortina close to the summit of Tofana di Mezzo. From the top, the view takes in the Cinque Torri, the Marmolada and the Cristallo.

Where to stay

Cortina d’Ampezzo. Compare the valleys in which Dolomites valley to stay in.

When to go

In winter the Tofane are Cortina’s main ski area, home to the Olympia delle Tofane World Cup run, used for women’s alpine skiing at the 2026 Winter Olympics. In summer, via ferratas and the Tofana di Rozes paths draw climbers and hikers.

Good to know

The Tofane are three summits: Tofana di Rozes, Tofana di Mezzo and Tofana di Dentro. Their huge south faces glow orange above Cortina at sunset.
